ABOUT US · OUR STORY

We are named after a promise: "Koode".

Koode (കൂടെ) means together, with you. What began as a small group of friends giving from their own salaries has grown into a charitable trust with one belief: no one facing hardship should feel they are alone.

We support people based on genuine need, never on religion, politics, community, region or caste. Our work covers healthcare, education and community support for families who need a helping hand.

"When someone genuinely needs a helping hand, we want to be there."
  1. 2015

    A group of friends

    We came together to help a child in our hometown with medical costs. It became a monthly commitment.

  2. 2022

    A turning point

    A friend's father, on dialysis, suffered a stroke. We saw how many families struggle to find affordable, continuous care.

  3. 2026

    A registered trust

    WITH YOU Charitable Trust was officially registered to provide long-term support, not just occasional help.

  4. Next

    Three or more centres

    After Kannur, we aim to open centres across Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

OUR INITIATIVE · FIRST MAJOR GOAL

A Dialysis & Physiotherapy Centre for Kannur.

Many patients struggle with the cost and availability of dialysis, physiotherapy and rehabilitation. Our centre will offer quality treatment at a cost people who can't afford private healthcare can actually manage.
